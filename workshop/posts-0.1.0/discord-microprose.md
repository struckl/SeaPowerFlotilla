**R.A.T.S. Flotilla – is it the game, or is it your mods?**
Something broke and you have 47 mods switched on. Good luck. Or:

• **One click, one bone-clean Sea Power** with no mods. Reproduce the bug there and the devs get a report they can use
• **Test a huge mod list on its own copy.** So loaded it's a miracle it starts? Your everyday game never finds out
• **Keep the old version** next to the new one after an update
• Every copy has its own mods, settings and saves, and your Steam install stays as it is

Small Windows app, about 19 GB per copy.
