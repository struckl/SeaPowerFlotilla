**R.A.T.S. Flotilla – is it the game, or is it your mods?**
Stop testing on the install you actually play.

Several Sea Power installs side by side, each with its own mods, settings and saves:
• **One click, one bone-clean Sea Power:** no mods, no loader. Reproduce the bug there and the devs get a report they can use
• **One copy per test:** try a build or a giant mod list. So loaded it's a miracle it starts? Your everyday game never finds out
• **Old and new game version** next to each other
• **Mods per copy:** on/off, load order, or add a folder or zip to just that copy
• Your Steam install stays as it is

Small Windows app, about 19 GB per copy.
